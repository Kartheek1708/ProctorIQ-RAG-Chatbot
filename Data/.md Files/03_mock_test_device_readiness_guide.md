# Mock-Test & Device Readiness Guide

## Overview

Every student is required to complete a mock test before their first proctored assessment on ProctorIQ, and it is strongly recommended before every subsequent assessment as well. The mock test exists to surface device, network, and webcam issues while there is still time to fix them.

## Section 1: What the Mock Test Checks

- **Webcam**: confirms your camera is detected, functional, and produces a clear image under your current lighting.
- **Microphone**: confirms audio input is detected (required for certain assessment formats with verbal components).
- **Network stability**: runs a short connectivity check to estimate whether your current connection can sustain a full assessment session.
- **Screen/display compatibility**: confirms your screen resolution and display setup are compatible with PSB's kiosk mode.
- **Face verification**: captures a reference image used for identity checks during the actual assessment.

## Section 2: Recommended Timing

Complete your mock test at least 24 hours before your scheduled assessment window. This is not a hard technical requirement, but it is the single most effective way to avoid last-minute issues — problems found during a mock test can be resolved with Support ahead of time; the same problems found five minutes before your exam window often cannot.

## Section 3: Common Mock-Test Failures and Fixes

### Webcam Not Detected
- Confirm no other application (video calling software, other browser tabs) is currently using your camera — most webcams can only be accessed by one application at a time.
- Check camera permissions are granted to PSB (see the Windows or Mac installation guide, Section 1).
- Try a different USB port if using an external webcam.

### Poor Lighting / Face Not Clearly Visible
- Sit facing a light source (window or lamp) rather than with it behind you.
- Avoid strong backlighting, which causes your face to appear as a silhouette.
- Remove sunglasses, hats, or anything obscuring your face during the verification capture.

### Network Check Fails or Shows "Unstable"
- Move closer to your Wi-Fi router if using wireless.
- Close other bandwidth-heavy applications (streaming, large downloads) before running the check.
- If consistently unstable, consider a wired Ethernet connection for your actual assessment — this is the most reliable option where available.

## Section 4: If Mock-Test Issues Persist

If you have followed the fixes above and the mock test still fails, contact the assessment support team through the in-platform Help option at least 24 hours before your exam window, providing the specific error shown and your device type (Windows/Mac, and OS version). Waiting until exam day to report an unresolved mock-test failure significantly reduces the chance of it being resolved before your assessment starts.
