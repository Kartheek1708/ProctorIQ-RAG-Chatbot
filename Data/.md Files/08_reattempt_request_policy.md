# Re-attempt Request Policy

## Overview

This document explains how re-attempt requests are handled when an assessment is interrupted due to a technical issue (network, device, or system-related). It does not cover re-attempts requested for reasons unrelated to a technical interruption.

## Section 1: Reporting an Interruption

If your assessment is interrupted due to a technical issue, report it to the assessment support team immediately — through the in-platform Help option if you are still able to access it, or through the alternate contact method provided in your original assessment invite if not.

Provide the following when reporting:
- The approximate time the interruption occurred.
- What you observed (e.g., "internet disconnected twice," "laptop became unresponsive and I had to restart").
- Your device type and operating system.

## Section 2: Why a Re-attempt Cannot Be Approved Instantly

A re-attempt cannot be approved at the time of the interruption, during the assessment, or immediately after it ends. This is not a delay in service — it reflects that a decision requires verifying what actually happened during your session (connectivity logs, device event logs, and the specific timing of any flags raised), which is not available for review until after the session has fully ended.

## Section 3: What the Review Considers

The support team verifies the assessment logs and technical details of the reported session before deciding whether a re-attempt can be provided. This includes cross-referencing the timing of any reported interruption against the system's own logs for that session.

## Section 4: What This Document Cannot Tell You

This document cannot confirm whether a specific re-attempt request will be approved — that depends on the outcome of the log review described above, on a case-by-case basis, and is communicated once that review is complete, not before. See the Status & Verification Outcome Guide for how to check on a pending request.
