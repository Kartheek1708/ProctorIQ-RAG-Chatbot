# During-Assessment Playbook: Device & System Issues

## Overview

This document covers what to do if your device becomes unresponsive or the assessment page freezes while an assessment is in progress.

## Section 1: Assessment Page Freezes or Gets Stuck

If the assessment page becomes unresponsive:
- Wait a few seconds first and check whether the page resumes automatically — many freezes are momentary.
- If the issue is due to your laptop itself becoming unresponsive (not just the assessment page), press and hold the power button to force a restart.
- After restarting, reopen the assessment using your original assessment link and continue, if the assessment window permits re-entry.

## Section 2: PSB Becomes Unresponsive (Not Just the Page)

If PSB itself is frozen (not just the content inside it):
- Wait 10-15 seconds before taking action — PSB occasionally pauses briefly during identity re-verification checks, which is expected behavior, not a freeze.
- If there is still no response after 15 seconds, force a restart of your device as described in Section 1.
- On reopening PSB, you will be returned to the assessment at your last saved point in most cases.

## Section 3: Low Battery or Power Loss

- Always assess on a device connected to power where possible; do not rely on battery alone for a full assessment session.
- If your device shuts down due to battery loss, reconnect power, restart the device, and reopen the assessment using your original link as soon as possible.

## Section 4: What This Document Does Not Cover

Whether a re-attempt is granted following a device or system interruption depends on review of your session logs by the support team — this cannot be determined at the moment the issue occurs. See the Re-attempt Request Policy document for that process. This document also does not cover camera, microphone, or face-verification warnings — see the Camera, Microphone & Face Verification Guide for those.
