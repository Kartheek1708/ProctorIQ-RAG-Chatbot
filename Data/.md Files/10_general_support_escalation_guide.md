# General Support & Escalation Guide

## Overview

This document covers general guidance for staying calm during a technical issue, what to do if a problem cannot be resolved through the phase-specific guides, and how to reach the assessment support team.

## Section 1: General Instructions During Any Issue

- Stay calm if you encounter a technical issue — most are recoverable, and panicking or repeatedly refreshing the page tends to make recovery harder, not easier.
- Do not repeatedly refresh the page or close the assessment unless a specific guide has instructed you to.
- Follow on-screen instructions carefully; they are often specific to the exact issue detected.
- If an issue cannot be resolved using the relevant phase-specific guide (installation, network, device, or camera/microphone), contact the assessment support team rather than continuing to retry indefinitely.

## Section 2: What to Provide When Contacting Support

To help resolve your issue as quickly as possible, provide:
- Your registered email or student ID.
- The specific assessment name and scheduled date/time.
- What you observed, as specifically as possible (exact error message, or a description of what happened and when).
- Your device type and operating system (Windows/Mac, and version if known).

## Section 3: What Support Can and Cannot Do in Real Time

Support can help you resolve installation, login, and device-readiness issues in real time, before your assessment begins. Support cannot, in real time and during an active assessment, approve a re-attempt, confirm the outcome of an integrity flag, or override the proctoring/integrity system — these all require the log-review process described in the Re-attempt Request Policy and the Status & Verification Outcome Guide, and are not decisions any individual support agent can make on the spot.

## Section 4: A Note on What Support Will Never Do

Support will never provide guidance intended to help a student bypass, disable, or reduce face verification, application-lock, or any other integrity control, regardless of the reason given. This applies during setup, during an assessment, and in any follow-up conversation after one — see the Permitted & Prohibited Actions Policy for the full boundary.
