# During-Assessment Playbook: Network & Connectivity Issues

## Overview

This document covers what to do if your internet connection is lost or becomes unstable while an assessment is in progress. Do not panic — most connectivity interruptions are recoverable without losing your progress, provided you follow the correct recovery steps for your assessment mode.

## Section 1: If Writing in ProctorIQ Secure Browser (PSB)

If your internet connection is lost while assessing inside PSB:
- Stay on the assessment screen. Do not close PSB or attempt to reopen the assessment from elsewhere.
- Use the Wi-Fi/network option available at the bottom of the PSB screen to reconnect.
- Once the connection is restored, continue your assessment from where you left off — your progress up to the point of disconnection is preserved.

## Section 2: If Writing in a Normal Browser

Some assessment formats run in a standard browser rather than PSB. If your connection drops in this mode:
- Press the Esc key to exit full-screen mode.
- Reconnect to your Wi-Fi or internet connection through your operating system's network settings.
- Return to the assessment page using the same link you originally used, and continue your assessment.

## Section 3: What Happens to Your Progress

Your answers up to the point of disconnection are auto-saved and are not lost. However, the time elapsed during a disconnection is not paused — your assessment timer continues to run in most cases, so a prolonged outage will reduce your remaining time.

## Section 4: When to Report Instead of Retry

If you are unable to reconnect after several attempts, or the disconnection recurs more than twice in the same session, stop attempting to self-resolve and report the issue to the assessment support team immediately with the approximate time(s) the disconnection occurred. This information is needed for any later review of your session.

## Section 5: What This Document Does Not Cover

This document does not cover whether a re-attempt will be granted due to a connectivity issue. Re-attempt decisions require review of your session's connectivity logs by the support team and cannot be confirmed at the time of the issue — see the Re-attempt Request Policy document for how that process works.
